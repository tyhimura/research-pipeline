AI infrastructure financing stress

Running page. Updated weekly by Sun.

Scope

How the AI buildout is paid for and where the financing strains show: hyperscaler free cash flow and capex guidance, bond and equity issuance, credit ratings, GPU leases and vendor financing, and neocloud access to capital. The story has run in Weekly Tech Updates since Weekly Tech Updates · 2026-10-10 (Broadcom's ~$60B debt package tied to Anthropic TPUs and Lambda's pre-IPO raise), continued in Weekly Tech Updates · 2026-10-17 (IMF warning on hyperscaler bond issuance) and Weekly Tech Updates · 2026-10-24 (Meta's negative free cash flow; neocloud capital scarcity), and was tracked inside Economic impact of AI until this page opened in week 4. Labor and productivity effects stay in that standing thread.
