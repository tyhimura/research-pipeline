Remote AI work: PH and beyond (running)

Running page. Updated weekly by Sun.

Scope

Remote and contract roles for AI professionals, with the Philippines first, then Southeast Asia and global. Covers hiring demand, pay rates, platforms, visa and digital-nomad rules, and the skills in demand. Distinct from AI, gig work and outsourcing, which covers AI displacing gig and BPO work, and from Economic impact of AI, which covers the broad economy. Standing thread, exempt from the 4-week graduation rule, with this page from day one. Unscored. One entry a week with 2–4 sourced bullets and a one-line "So what"; a one-line "Quiet week" note when there is no news. Big stories run in full in a lane or in Top Hits, with a one-line pointer here. No stock tips.

Weekly log

  • Nov 26, 2026 (week of Nov 19–26) — Weekly Tech Updates · 2026-11-26
    • Malaysia's enhanced DE Rantau nomad pass starts Dec 1: the tech-talent income floor rises from $24,000 to $36,000 a year, total stays can reach four years, and holders may work for up to three overseas clients or employers; new applications are paused until then. The Malaysia Move
    • The Philippines' digital nomad visa, authorised by Executive Order 86 in April 2025, is still not open for applications more than a year later. Nomad Atlas
    • An ADB study of six million job ads in six economies, including the Philippines and Malaysia, finds over 85% ask for basic digital skills and over 10% for advanced ones, and that demand for AI skills is following the same spread. East Asia Forum
    • On the demand side, enterprises report using fewer offshore contractors as AI takes on work (see AI, gig work and outsourcing). Anthropic
    • So what: first entry, as a baseline: Malaysia is raising the bar to attract higher-paid tech nomads while the Philippines still has no working remote-work visa, and buyers of offshore work are getting pickier.
