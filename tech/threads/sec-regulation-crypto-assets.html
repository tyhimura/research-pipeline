Narrative · SEC Regulation Crypto Assets

Running page. Updated weekly by Sun.

📌

Ongoing narrative spanning more than 4 weeks. Linked from Weekly Tech Updates. Not investment advice.

Status

SEC proposed Regulation Crypto Assets on Aug 18, 2026 (release 2026-76). Public comment remains open for 60 days after Federal Register publication (into late October 2026).

What it does

Creates a tailored Securities Act offering regime for certain investment contracts involving crypto assets, building on the Commission’s March 2026 interpretive guidance.

  • One-time exemption: offerings up to $5 million over a four-year period, with principles-based narrative disclosures.
  • Larger exemption: offerings up to $75 million per 12-month period, with financial statements and ongoing reporting.
  • Conditional safe harbor from “investment contract” once specified managerial-effort conditions are met.
  • Preempts certain state registration/qualification requirements for covered offerings and some secondary trades.

Why it stays open

Comment-period clocks and any final rulemaking will shape U.S. onshore capital formation for crypto issuers for years. Watch Federal Register publication date, comment letters, and any Congressional interaction with a lasting statutory framework.
