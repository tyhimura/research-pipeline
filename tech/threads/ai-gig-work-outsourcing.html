AI, gig work and outsourcing (running)

Running page. Updated weekly by Sun.

Scope

Freelance platforms and gig marketplaces: shifts in demand and pay rates, Upwork and Fiverr data, and AI-agent marketplaces. BPO, meaning call centres, back office, content moderation and data annotation: AI replacing or augmenting work, contract losses, and hubs like the Philippines and India, with Southeast Asian and Thai angles welcome. Jobs and economic data, policy, and reskilling responses for these sectors. Distinct from Economic impact of AI, which covers the broad economy; this thread covers these sectors specifically. Standing thread, exempt from the 4-week graduation rule, with this page from day one. Unscored. One entry a week; a one-line "Quiet week" note when there is no news. Big stories run in full in a lane or in Top Hits, with a one-line pointer here. No stock tips.
