AI, gig work and outsourcing (running)

Running page. Updated weekly by Sun.

Scope

Freelance platforms and gig marketplaces: shifts in demand and pay rates, Upwork and Fiverr data, and AI-agent marketplaces. BPO, meaning call centres, back office, content moderation and data annotation: AI replacing or augmenting work, contract losses, and hubs like the Philippines and India, with Southeast Asian and Thai angles welcome. Jobs and economic data, policy, and reskilling responses for these sectors. Distinct from Economic impact of AI, which covers the broad economy; this thread covers these sectors specifically. Standing thread, exempt from the 4-week graduation rule, with this page from day one. Unscored. One entry a week; a one-line "Quiet week" note when there is no news. Big stories run in full in a lane or in Top Hits, with a one-line pointer here. No stock tips.

Weekly log

  • Nov 26, 2026 (week of Nov 19–26) — Weekly Tech Updates · 2026-11-26
    • Anthropic's enterprise customers report cutting contractors, especially offshore ones, which Anthropic calls a possible early warning for the international business-services industry (see Economic impact of AI). Anthropic
    • In Cebu, a BPO team lead hired in 2008 says entry-level pay has barely moved for years while employers now ask for adaptability and digital skills, and repetitive work is the most exposed to automation. SunStar Cebu
    • In remarks reported Nov 24, Labor Secretary Francis Tolentino told Philippine employers that AI will displace some jobs and must not come at the expense of workers' rights, and urged upskilling and workforce planning. People Matters
    • So what: the first enterprise survey evidence of offshore contractor cuts landed the same week Philippine officials started talking about displacement openly. No new hard sector jobs data this week.
  • Nov 19, 2026 (week of Nov 12–19) — Weekly Tech Updates · 2026-11-19
    • AWS is pitching Amazon WorkSpaces as the place for agents to handle ERP transactions and work through claims queues, the same back-office work that BPO firms sell. AWS
    • About 20.7% of Filipinos aged 15 to 64 used generative AI in Q2 2026, per Microsoft's AI Economy Institute. That ranks the Philippines, the largest BPO hub, fourth in Southeast Asia, behind Singapore, Vietnam and Malaysia. Inquirer
    • So what: vendors are aiming agents squarely at queue-based back-office work, while the biggest BPO workforce is only mid-pack on everyday AI use. No new hard jobs data on these sectors this week.
