AI in Finance (running)

Running page. Updated weekly by Sun.

Scope

Quant and trading uses of AI; bank and asset-manager adoption; AI fintech products (incl. agentic payments and checkout); regulator moves on AI in markets; AI-linked market risks; and AI agents in crypto. Standing thread, exempt from the 4-week graduation rule. Unscored. No stock tips.

Weekly log

  • Oct 31, 2026 (week of Oct 24–31) — Weekly Tech Updates · 2026-10-31
    • Bank of America emailed customers not to give AI agents their banking passwords, and said customers who do are responsible for the resulting activity (Markets, 6). TechCrunch
    • DoorDash: outside AI agents have generated only "several hundred" orders, against just under 1B orders in Q3 (Markets, 5). Ars Technica
    • Coinbase: prediction-market revenue passed $200M annualized, traders on those markets rose 60% q/q, and Coinbase for Agents launched in early October (Crypto, 6). CNBC
    • Korea's FSS governor warned that AI makes it easier for hackers to find vulnerabilities and launch simultaneous attacks, as breaches spread to Daishin, Bithumb and Mirae Asset (Cybersecurity, 6). The Korea Herald
    • So what: agent commerce is still tiny in volume, and banks are pushing liability for unsanctioned agents onto customers. Sanctioned, scoped agent access remains the gating product.
  • Oct 24, 2026 (week of Oct 17–24) — Weekly Tech Updates · 2026-10-24
    • Morgan Stanley is blocking unauthorized third-party AI agents that log into customer accounts, while JPMorgan, Bank of America and Citi are restricting them (Markets, 7). Axios
    • Stripe Treasury for agents: ChatGPT or Claude agents use Stripe's MCP server, with access set per account, a read-or-act choice, and human approval to move money (Markets, 7). Stripe
    • Visa, BILL and Billtrust started the first live B2B pilot of agent payments on the Trusted Agent Protocol (Markets, 6). Visa
    • So what: banks are blocking agents they haven't vetted while payment networks build sanctioned rails, so agent identity and scoped access are becoming the control point.
  • Oct 17, 2026 (week of Oct 10–17) — Weekly Tech Updates · 2026-10-17
    • Pointer: JPMorgan confirms it blocks external AI agents acting for retail customers — Fintech (7). CNBC
    • Pointer: Bank of America brought in 1,000 contractors plus 3,000 staff to fix flaws Anthropic's Mythos surfaced — Cybersecurity (7). CNBC
    • Pointer: Citadel's research agent replicates a finance paper in 2–3 hours versus 6–8 weeks manually — Quant (6). TMC Insight
    • Pointer: Chainalysis counts 138M+ agent payments over x402 on Base, 87%+ under 10 cents — Crypto (6). Decrypt
    • So what: big banks are fencing off customer accounts from third-party agents while spending heavily on AI internally, both to cut costs and to clean up what AI scanners find. Agent identity and payments are maturing outside bank rails.
  • Oct 10, 2026 (week of Oct 3–10) — Weekly Tech Updates · 2026-10-10
    • Jump Trading: multi-day agent “autoresearch” on GPT-6 Astra, gated by scoped monitored environments and human acceptance review. OpenAI
    • Crossmint Agent Commerce Toolkit: one API for agent card checkout using Visa Intelligent Commerce and Mastercard Agent Pay credentials. PR Newswire
    • Constructor × Stripe agentic checkout inside on-site AI shopping agents. PR Newswire
    • Anthropic’s Usage Policy rewrite restates human-in-the-loop plus disclosure for uses affecting someone’s finances (effective Nov 12). No regulator action on AI in markets and no material AI-agent-in-crypto news this week. Anthropic
    • So what: agentic finance is in production at both ends (quant research, consumer checkout), with vendor and card-network controls standing in for regulation.
