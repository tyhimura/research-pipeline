Anthropic IPO

Running page. Updated weekly by Sun.

Scope

Anthropic's path to a public listing: disclosed financials, the S-1 and its amendments, pricing, the listing, lock-ups and post-IPO credit ratings. The story has run in Weekly Tech Updates since Q3 revenue was disclosed to investors and a November listing was reported (see Weekly Tech Updates · 2026-10-17, second week). It continued as a rumor of an imminent public filing in Weekly Tech Updates · 2026-10-24, and this page opened in week 4, when the public S-1 was filed. The legal fight with the Pentagon has its own page: Anthropic vs. Department of War supply-chain-risk case.

Weekly log

  • Nov 12, 2026 (weeks of Oct 31–Nov 12) — Weekly Tech Updates · 2026-11-12
    • Nov 10: the roadshow opened for 825M Class A shares at $70–78, of which 801.4M are new shares, plus a 123.75M-share underwriter option. Anthropic
    • CNBC puts proceeds at up to $64.35B and the fully diluted value at up to $1.405T. People familiar say pricing is Nov 18, trading starts Nov 19, and retail gets about 20%. CNBC
    • The IPO paperwork shows September-quarter sales of $16.9B, up 46% on the quarter, and a $567M operating loss, as relayed by MarketWatch. Morningstar (MarketWatch)
    • So what: MarketWatch reports the range implies less than some investors had hoped, so the AI selloff has already reset what public markets will pay for a frontier lab.
  • Oct 31, 2026 (week of Oct 24–31) — Weekly Tech Updates · 2026-10-31
    • Oct 26: Anthropic publicly filed its S-1 to list Class A shares on the Nasdaq as "ANT". The share count and price range have not been set, and Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are the lead underwriters (Top Hits, 9). Anthropic
    • From the prospectus: H1 2026 revenue of $16.29B (+1,304%), a net loss of $80.7B (about $72B of it convertible-note charges), net revenue retention around 900%, and compute commitments with SpaceX, Amazon, Google and Microsoft. A founder LLC holds 50.1% of the voting power, and lock-ups open in stages at 27, 48, 62 and 76 days. CNBC
    • Rumor: Blackstone now expects a "high" junk (double-B) rating after the IPO (FT via TechCrunch). TechCrunch
    • So what: the S-1 gives the first audited look at frontier-lab economics. Watch for the price range, the listing date and how the compute commitments are treated by rating agencies.
