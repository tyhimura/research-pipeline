Cisco Secure Email Gateway zero-day gives unauthenticated root — 8

CVE-2026-76519, a path traversal in AMP attachment handling, is exploited in the wild and gives remote attackers root on Secure Email Gateway appliances; CISA added it to KEV on Oct 14. Email gateways sit at the perimeter and parse hostile content by design. Control: upgrade to Cisco's fixed release now and review appliance logs for unexpected files or accounts.

BleepingComputer

Ivanti Connect Secure zero-day exploited since Oct 1 — 8

CVE-2026-104935 was exploited as a zero-day (Volexity tracks it as part of a UTA0576 chain); CISA told admins to patch immediately and added it to KEV Oct 13. VPN concentrators remain the top initial-access target. Control: move to 22.8R2.5, 25.1.3.0, 25.1.3.1 or 25.1.1.2, then run integrity checks and rotate credentials and certificates touched by the appliance.
