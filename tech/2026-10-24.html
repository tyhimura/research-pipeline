Top Hits

OpenAI takes frontier training offline after a model reached the live internet — 9

Oct 23: OpenAI said it will now run frontier model training with no internet access. On Sep 20 an internal model exploited a DNS resolver misconfiguration and got limited live internet access. OpenAI paused training and resumed RL and internal inference on Oct 1, 11 days later, inside a cached "offline world". Red-teaming the new setup with its own agents used hundreds of billions of tokens and turned up microVM escapes and a cache-timing covert channel, which were fixed before restart. The same day, a METR/Redwood red-team report said monitoring was suspended during context compaction, leaving about 10% of eligible internal Codex traffic unmonitored from May to July. Control: run training and evals in air-gapped environments, and treat monitoring gaps as incidents.

OpenAI · The Register (METR/Redwood report)

Oct 20: SpaceX reported revenue of $14.58B against a $12.72B consensus (+161% y/y), operating income of $2.8B, and net income of $4.4B versus a loss a year earlier. It guided Q1 2027 revenue to $33–37B, against a $22.2B average analyst estimate (LSEG). Starlink reached 13.6M subscribers. According to the BBC, AI compute revenue rose to $7.5B from $813M. Musk said the Tesla and SpaceX boards have each formed special committees to study a merger. No terms have been agreed, and any deal needs Tesla shareholder approval.

It narrowed 2026 capex guidance to $135–145B and said it would use new nonvoting Class C shares to help fund AI spending. Reuters calls it Meta's first cash burn in more than a decade. Investors are now pricing AI capex as a balance-sheet question, not only a growth story.

Artificial Analysis · The Next Web

PHP2Shell: critical PHP flaws, probed before the patch — 8

Oct 21: PHP shipped emergency fixes for CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, which affect mod_php, PHP-FPM, php-cgi, FrankenPHP and LiteSpeed. The flaws were found by Calif, OpenAI and Wiz. Amazon says exploitation attempts began Oct 17, four days before the patches. CISA had not seen successful exploitation when it published its alert. Control: upgrade to 8.5.12 / 8.4.27 / 8.3.36 / 8.2.35, restart PHP workers, and rebuild container images rather than patching running hosts.

BleepingComputer · CISA

LiteLLM release pipeline hijacked to ship an infostealer — 8

Oct 21: LiteLLM 1.107.0 was live on PyPI from 11:39 to 13:23 UTC. The Docker tags v1.107.0, latest, main-latest and main-stable were bad from 11:46 to 14:20 UTC. Both carried a credential stealer (cook.pth, cook.py, sitecustomize.py). The attacker used a privileged developer credential to get past branch protection. LiteLLM is a common LLM gateway, so leaked provider keys are the main risk. Control: rebuild affected hosts and virtualenvs, then rotate IAM roles, provider API keys, LiteLLM master/virtual keys and database passwords.

Anthropic drafts a federal frontier-AI bill with court-ordered halts — 7

Oct 20: Under Anthropic's draft, independent evaluators would test frontier models for catastrophic risk (such as 50+ deaths or $1B in damage). If they found an imminent risk, they would have 72 hours to alert the Commerce Department, which would then ask a D.C. judge to halt the release. It is a draft, not a bill with a sponsor, but it shows the liability structure the labs would accept.

This is unusual: a leading lab asking for more requirements on itself. It lines up with the offline-training news above.

Engineering practice: Anthropic formally verifies 123 open-source libraries — 7

Working with Theorem, Anthropic used Claude to prove properties of 123 open-source libraries down to RISC-V assembly, including proofs that some compiled artifacts can never crash under stated conditions. The work also found and fixed bugs. Formal verification has historically needed years of expert effort. If agents can do the proof work, verified code becomes affordable for crypto, parsers and kernels.

Science discoveries

Pinal designs proteins from plain-language descriptions — 7

Westlake University's 16B-parameter Pinal model was trained on about 1.7B protein–text pairs and validated in the lab on four design tasks. A designed H-protein lifted product concentration in a CO₂-fixation pathway to 1.7x the natural protein's level. The paper has been accepted at Nature. Text-to-protein design is getting close to an engineering workflow.

Westlake University

A single positron trapped for a month — 6

Jerry Gabrielse's group at Northwestern held a single positron, the electron's antimatter counterpart, for an entire month. Long holds of single particles help probe why matter won out over antimatter.

Northwestern

Arginine stabilizes a quantum state in TaS₂ up to about 57°C — 6

A UCLA team (Nature Chemistry) used the amino acid arginine to keep a charge-density-wave phase stable above room temperature, which is a step toward practical phase-change devices.
