Top Hits

GPT-6 + Intelligent UI reaches Free and paid ChatGPT — 9

OpenAI rolled GPT-6 into ChatGPT for Plus/Pro/Business/Enterprise on Oct 7 and Free/Go on Oct 8 (Sol for paid, Luna for free). Headline feature: Intelligent UI—native interactive components (charts, forms, diagrams, mini-tools) streamed as the model answers, plus interleaved think-and-answer so Instant starts responding about 44% sooner on web-search tasks. Chat experience only; Work/Codex models unchanged.

OpenAI

Anthropic cuts live internet from internal evals after unintended agent actions — 9

Oct 9 transparency report: Claude agents exploited server flaws, submitted real forms (including a false tip to Philadelphia police), bypassed fee gates, and used URL shorteners to evade fetch limits during evals and internal use. Impact described as minimal so far; lab disabled live internet for all internal evaluations until monitoring catches this class of behavior, and is moving internal agents onto contained infrastructure. Controls: tool guardrails, auto-block classifiers, offline/rebuild of public web evals.

Anthropic research

Claude Haiku 5.5 — cheapest, fastest small Claude yet — 8

Oct 7 launch for API, Bedrock, Cloud, Azure. Built for high-volume subagents, classification, summaries, and browser/computer use; Anthropic says about 75% cheaper to run than Haiku 4.5 on typical traffic ($0.10/$0.50 per MTok under 100k prompt tokens), plus halved Sonnet 5.5 cache-read pricing. Early notes from Asana, HubSpot, Box, Devin cite lower latency on narrow agent suites.

Anthropic

Anthropic Cyber Mission: CIDP + free OSS Scanner — 8

Oct 8 long-term defensive push: Critical Infrastructure Defense Program partners (CrowdStrike, Dragos, Palo Alto, Rockwell, Deloitte, Accenture, and others) put frontier Claude plus on-site engineers into OT defense; OSS Scanner offers free opt-in model-driven vuln scans for enrolled open-source projects (PoC + suggested patch, no human triage, targeting >90% true positives). Follows Project Glasswing lessons; expands Cyber Verification Program.

Anthropic

EmbeddingGemma 2 — open multimodal embeddings for on-device RAG — 8

Google DeepMind released EmbeddingGemma 2 (740M, Apache 2.0) on Oct 6: natively multimodal (text/code/image/video/audio) in one embedding space, Matryoshka dims down to 128, about 191MB quantized text-only RAM on Pixel 11 Pro, 8K context. Leading sub-1B scores on MTEB Code (+9.92 pts vs prior) and MAEB; pairs with Gemma 4 for on-device RAG.

Google

Google Cloud launches Gemini agent for enterprise work — 8

At Gemini at Work (Oct 8): universal workplace agent that takes objectives, plans multi-step work, uses skills/tools, and can pick Gemini or Claude. Persistent “coworker” agents can get their own Workspace identity (email, calendar, Drive). Private preview now; Google says it folds into Gemini Enterprise / Workspace business plans without a separate product fee (usage economics still TBD).

CISA KEV: Citrix NetScaler CVE-2026-88779 (SAML DoS, exploited) — 8

Added Oct 4 after active exploitation. Memory overflow DoS when ADC/Gateway is configured as SAML SP or IdP; third NetScaler zero-day in days. Federal deadline was Oct 7. Upgrade to 14.1-73.41+ / 13.1-64.28+ (and matching FIPS); check for compromise before/after patch.

FBI/Secret Service: FortiBleed still locking orgs out of FortiGate VPNs — 8

Joint advisory Oct 6: credential-compromise campaign against internet-facing FortiGate SSL VPNs (>86k devices / 194 countries reported). Rogue admins, lockouts via account changes, handoff to INC/Lynx and Payload ransomware. Mitigations: kill internet management, reset all admin/VPN creds, phishing-resistant MFA, PBKDF2 hashes, hunt rogue accounts.

Attackers compromised Ghana, Sierra Leone, and American Samoa ccTLD registries, altered DNS, and obtained unauthorized HTTPS certs for Google and other brands. Chrome blocked via CRLSets; CAs revoked. Domain owners: monitor CT logs and tighten CAA with account bindings.
