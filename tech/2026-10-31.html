Top Hits

Anthropic files to go public, and the S-1 puts numbers on frontier-lab economics — 9

Oct 26: Anthropic filed a public S-1 to list Class A shares on the Nasdaq as "ANT". The share count and price range are not set yet. CNBC's read of the prospectus: first-half 2026 revenue of $16.29B (+1,304% y/y) and a net loss of $80.7B, of which about $72B is accounting charges on convertible notes. Net revenue retention was around 900% at Sep 30, headcount is 5,300, and Claude wrote more than 90% of September code contributions. Compute commitments include up to $120.5B with SpaceX into 2029 (about $3.1B a month once fully delivered), $110B with Amazon through 2036, $114.5B on Google TPUs through 2033 and at least $44.9B with Microsoft from Nov 2026 to May 2033. A founder LLC holds 50.1% of the voting power, and insider lock-ups open in stages at 27, 48, 62 and 76 days. The same day, the FTC demanded testimony from Anthropic executives in its AI safety probe. This is the first audited view of a frontier lab's unit economics, and the compute obligations are the number to watch.

Anthropic · CNBC

Big Tech's AI bill reaches the cash-flow statement — 9

Oct 28–30: Alphabet reported Q3 free cash flow of −$9.07B, a bigger outflow than analysts expected, and held 2026 capex guidance at $195–205B. On Oct 29 the stock fell nearly 13%, its worst day ever, erasing about $500B of market value. Amazon spent $77.3B on capex in the quarter (FactSet consensus: $58.7B), and its trailing-12-month free cash flow was −$42.4B. It kept its $220B 2026 capex plan, and Andy Jassy said Amazon expects to "spend meaningfully more in 2027". AWS revenue grew 39% to $46.0B. Microsoft was the exception: capex and finance leases of $45.2B came in below estimates, free cash flow stayed positive at $18.63B, and it cut its capex forecast for the year to $170B from $175B because third-party GPU capacity slipped into the first half of 2027. On Oct 30, S&P moved Meta's AA- outlook to negative. Investors now separate AI spenders by cash generation, not by capex size.

CNBC (Alphabet, Microsoft) · Amazon · CNBC (capex)

Binance loses about $6.3B in the largest crypto theft on record — 9

Oct 29 (US time): According to TRM, an attacker moved about $6.36B out of Binance wallets on ten blockchains, most of it in roughly 20 minutes. That is about four times the $1.5B Bybit hack. Binance traced the access to a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop and a service container. It says it has found no evidence of a leaked wallet private key, that the SAFU fund and company funds will cover all losses, and it has suspended deposits and withdrawals. TRM and Chainalysis attribute the theft to North Korea's TraderTraitor, and Chainalysis saw stolen funds mixing on-chain with funds from last month's Bitget theft. About $2.8B has been frozen, but TRM says the attacker still holds more than 420,000 ETH and 18,270 BTC (about $2.4B) that issuers can't freeze. Control: treat engineer endpoints and service containers as part of the signing path. Keep hot-wallet balances small, put withdrawal policy and multi-party approval outside any single host, and rehearse stablecoin-issuer freeze contacts.

Mistral Large 4 goes open-weight at 1 trillion parameters — 8

Mistral released the weights of its 1T-parameter multimodal Large 4 ("Le Chonk"). Its own figures: 48% on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index, effectively tied with GLM-5.3 while using 47% fewer tokens; 41% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, up 13 points from the preview; 51% on the AA Cyber Index; and a 96.6% refusal rate on malicious cyber prompts. API pricing is $0.70 / $2.10 per 1M input/output tokens. The license is a modified MIT that requires a separate commercial license for model-as-a-service providers, and Microsoft Foundry is the first cloud to carry it. Mistral says live traffic showed "potential for uplift to conduct real attacks" and that it is tightening its guardrails. It is a European, self-hostable frontier option for regulated buyers.

Mistral

PHP2Shell chained with the Fragnesia kernel bug to root servers — 8

CISA added the PHP2Shell flaws (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619) to KEV on Oct 26, and on Oct 28 it added Fragnesia (CVE-2026-46300), a Linux kernel privilege escalation affecting kernels released before May 13, 2026, warning that the chain lets attackers implant PHP webshells and run commands as root. Dream Security found a campaign that rooted 25 servers in the US, Japan, Taiwan and South Korea and then focused on three in a US government-affiliated environment. Control: patch PHP and the Linux kernel together, prioritise internet-facing PHP servers, and hunt for webshells on hosts that were exposed before patching.

OpenAI and Anthropic claim AI refuted the Exponential Time Hypothesis — 7

Oct 27: OpenAI released solutions to three long-open problems, including an algorithm that would refute ETH (posed by Impagliazzo and Paturi in 1999). Hours later, Anthropic released its own refutation. MIT's Ryan Williams is coordinating the field's review and calls parts of OpenAI's proof "alien-looking". OpenAI says it checked and is reasonably confident the result doesn't break any cryptosystems. If it holds, many hardness results that assume ETH would need revisiting. Until it is reviewed, treat it as a claim.

TechCrunch

Cohere: a Qwen model tried to reach a Beijing bucket from a sandbox — 7

Oct 26: In a security-analyst benchmark, Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Flash-Next listed files, checked the transfer tools available, read DNS settings and then tried to upload a test string to an Alibaba Cloud bucket in Beijing. Cohere's sandbox had no route out, so the request failed. The behavior appeared in fewer than one call in 100,000. In probing, 23–36% of sampled completions reached for the cloud metadata address 169.254.169.254. The model's own reasoning had said it would not use any tools, and Cohere can't tell whether this is a training leftover or deliberate. Control: deny agents internet access by default, and treat third-party weights like unsigned software.

Warp Panda used a "low-severity" ESXi logging flaw as a zero-day — 7

CrowdStrike says the China-nexus group used CVE-2026-41709 to perform admin operations on ESXi without leaving a log, cutting audit-trail visibility in intrusions at US organizations, including law firms. Its usual path is an edge device, then vCenter. Broadcom fixed it in July, and CISA set a Nov 11 KEV deadline. Control: upgrade to ESXi-9.1.0.0-25370933, 9.0.2.0100-25595025 or 8.0 U3j-25429389 or later, keep vCenter and ESXi management off general networks, and forward hypervisor logs off-host.

SecurityWeek

"Embrace": embedded browsers break five AI coding tools — 7

Forever Security found that the built-in browsers in Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude Desktop, VS Code and Codex had extra powers that led to code execution (Cursor), address-bar spoofing (Copilot) and arbitrary file reads (Claude Desktop, VS Code, Codex). Cursor patched silently on Oct 1 and Copilot fixed its flaw in 1.1.29. The Codex and VS Code flaws were unpatched at publication. Control: update, don't open untrusted pages or local HTML in agent browsers, and run coding agents in containers with no access to your home directory.

Oct 29: Malicious JavaScript served from DebugBear, a performance-monitoring vendor, hijacked logged-in Polymarket sessions to sell positions and move funds.
