Power Rankings · week of 2026-10-31

Updated every Saturday as part of Weekly Tech Updates.

Rubric: each company gets a 0–100 score from six components graded 0–10: power now (moat 20, scale 15, talent 15) and impact on where tech is going (frontier 20, ecosystem 15, momentum 15). Ties go to the impact subtotal, then momentum, and weekly moves are capped at ±5 points.

Week of 2026-10-31

Week 3: every score is held at its Oct 24 level. Under the rubric a score moves only on a cited tier-1 event, and none had been verified when scores froze at 09:00 Bangkok time, so each reason below is the evidence behind the current score. Last week's tier-1 candidates (SpaceX Q3, Meta free cash flow, OpenAI, Tesla Optimus, Moonshot K3.1) remain under review and move nothing until a verified source is loaded.

The list still has 48 rows. xAI and Cursor stay folded into SpaceX under the rule that parent companies include their subsidiaries, and the pending candidate additions are not added until Tyler picks.

Valuation flag rule: A company is flagged as underpriced power when all four hold. (1) Its valuation rank minus its power rank is at least 3 places and at least one third of its sector group, rounded up. (2) Its power rank is in the top half of its group. (3) The group has at least 5 public names. (4) At least 2 data sources agree on its valuation inputs. (Warren)

Within each sector group, valuation rank 1 is the richest by EV to forward sales (forward sales = next-twelve-months consensus revenue, a time-weighted blend of current and next fiscal-year estimates), with forward P/E as a cross-check for profitable companies. A positive gap means the market prices the company below its power. A company priced well above its power is noted as 'rich' but isn't flagged. Every flag gets a one-line bear case. Private companies show no valuation and no gap. Warren's view, not investment advice. Blank-free cells: 'not flagged', 'n/a (private)', 'n/a (loss-making)' (negative or no forward earnings) and 'n/a (single source)' are explicit values.
