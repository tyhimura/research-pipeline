Power Rankings · week of 2026-11-12

Updated every Thursday as part of Weekly Tech Updates.

Rubric: each company gets a 0–100 score from six components graded 0–10: power now (moat 20, scale 15, talent 15) and impact on where tech is going (frontier 20, ecosystem 15, momentum 15). Ties go to the impact subtotal, then momentum, and weekly moves are capped at ±5 points.

Week of 2026-11-12

Week 4 (first Thursday edition, covering Oct 31 to Nov 12): ten scores move, each on a cited tier-1 event and within the ±5 cap. Nvidia drops 4.5 points on the board investigation into its CEO and falls to #3; Anthropic (+3.0), OpenAI (+1.5) and AMD (+3.0) rise on launches and results, and last week's verified candidates (SK hynix, Samsung, Intel, ServiceNow, Mistral, CoreWeave) are now applied. Alphabet is held: record-low cash flow and its worst trading day offset the Gemini 4 Carbon launch.

The list still has 48 rows. xAI and Cursor stay folded into SpaceX under the rule that parent companies include their subsidiaries, and the pending candidate additions are not added until Tyler picks. Moonshot AI's Kimi K3.1 Pro weights shipped on schedule, but news about Chinese companies needs a second confirming source, so its score is held.

Valuation flag rule: A company is flagged as underpriced power when all four hold. (1) Its valuation rank minus its power rank is at least 3 places and at least one third of its sector group, rounded up. (2) Its power rank is in the top half of its group. (3) The group has at least 5 public names. (4) At least 2 data sources agree on its valuation inputs. (Warren)

Within each sector group, valuation rank 1 is the richest by EV to forward sales (forward sales = next-twelve-months consensus revenue, a time-weighted blend of current and next fiscal-year estimates), with forward P/E as a cross-check for profitable companies. A positive gap means the market prices the company below its power. A company priced well above its power is noted as 'rich' but isn't flagged. Every flag gets a one-line bear case. Private companies show no valuation and no gap. Warren's view, not investment advice. Blank-free cells: 'not flagged', 'n/a (private)', 'n/a (loss-making)' (negative or no forward earnings) and 'n/a (single source)' are explicit values. Nov 12 refresh: prices are the Nov 11 close (Yahoo Finance); market cap and EV for the flagged names were rechecked against stockanalysis.com share counts and net cash, and other names are repriced from Oct 30 with revenue estimates carried forward (no name near a flag threshold was left unchecked).
