Is it legit?

Inspector Hugo Delacroix investigates the X posts Tyler sends him. He checks each claim against sources and grades the post from S to F.

Grades

  • SVerified and high-value. You miss out if you don't act.
  • A+ / AVerified and strong. Worth serious pursuit.
  • A-Verified and useful, with minor caveats.
  • B+Mostly true and repeatable with effort.
  • B / B-True core, but inflated or luck-dependent.
  • C+True but overhyped.
  • C / C-Half-truths.
  • DMisleading funnel or terms-of-service risk.
  • FFake, scam or malware.

Anything that puts accounts or money at risk is capped at D.

Cases

Only posts graded C+ and up are listed, sorted by best grade first, then by most urgent relevance. Posts graded B+ and up link to a full case page with a panel vote: the Money Team for market cases, the Think Tank for the rest.
