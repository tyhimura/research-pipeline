Definitions. Swing trade means about 2–8 weeks. Position means about 3–12 months. Return is price change from the logged entry to the exit, or to the last price if still open. Excess is that return minus QQQ over the same dates. A hit is positive excess return at horizon end or at exit. Gap closed is scored separately from price: did the market close the gap between power score and valuation that Warren flagged (yes, partly, or no), judged on the next Rankings after the idea closes.
Market Watch picks come from /workspace/market-watch/YYYY-MM-DD/picks.json (price, Warren's conservative value, discount, catalyst), one record per pick, tracked from the pick date. Return is in local currency. vs local is return minus the pick's local index; vs QQQ is return minus QQQ. Discount closed is how much of the gap between the pick price and Warren's value has been made up: (price minus pick price) divided by (value minus pick price), so 100% means value reached. The sell trigger fires when price reaches Warren's value or he says the thesis broke; the pick then moves to Sold. At 3, 6, 12 and 24 months after the pick, its return, vs local, vs QQQ and discount closed are frozen as a checkpoint (sold picks keep their exit result for later checkpoints). Hit means beating the local index at that checkpoint.
Not advice. These are research ideas for Tyler's review. Nothing here was traded.
