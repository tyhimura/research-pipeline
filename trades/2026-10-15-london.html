Pre-trade checklist

  1. Account: how much room is left to the max loss today? Is risk about $100 on 1 micro? (Balance ~$24,775.54; max loss $24,000; profit target $27,000.)
  2. Daily rules: fewer than 3 losses today? Not straight after 2 losses in a row (30-minute break)? Profit lock not yet reached?
  3. News: any release in the next 30 minutes? Respect the no-trade zone from 5 minutes before to 2 minutes after. Today: Breeden ~14:45 BKK — flat 14:40–14:47; PPI/retail 19:30 — flat leftovers by 19:25.
  4. Bias: is the higher-timeframe and macro bias clear? Does this trade agree with it, or is it a quick scalp only?
  5. Level: is the entry at a marked level (opening range, POC, value area, Asia high or low, a 30m or 15m level)?
  6. Setup: which setup type is it (breakout, fade, pullback, order flow, news)? Tag it.
  7. Plan: are the entry, stop, 1R partial and at-least-1.5R target all set before you click?
  8. State: calm and not chasing? If not, skip it.

Mindfulness reset (about 60 seconds, before the session and after any loss)

  • Feet flat, hands off the mouse.
  • Breathe in for 4 seconds, hold for 4, out for 6. Do this 5 times.
  • Name what you feel in one word, such as "eager", "frustrated" or "calm". Just notice it.
  • Say your plan out loud in one sentence: "I'm only taking ___ at ___, and I'll risk $100."
  • If the word was "frustrated" or "eager", wait 5 minutes before the first trade.
