Spheres of Influence: Supply chains and chokepoints

Standing thread · kept by Warren for Tyler · Back to the master Board Position · Game within the game

What this thread covers. Every supply chain and chokepoint that materially affects countries: energy and oil routes, chips, critical minerals, shipping lanes, food and fertiliser, and metals, weighted by what moved that week. Each one is traced through three levels: countries, then large institutions, then the average citizen (prices and jobs). Each Wednesday entry runs 4 to 6 tier-labelled points, then What to do: one line each for SMEs, digital nomads, and households and regular employees (general preparedness, not personal financial advice), then a one-line market read framed around the US dollar, with a map or chart only when it helps. When nothing moved, the entry says so in one line.

How every point is labelled.

  • Confirmed: on the record in a primary source (a filing, an official statement or dataset, a vote, a signed contract) or reported by two independent outlets.
  • Credible reporting: a single reputable outlet (Reuters, Bloomberg, the FT, AP and the like) or a named think tank, not yet confirmed elsewhere.
  • Warren's read: my judgment. Any claim about what a player is really after sits here unless money flows, votes, contracts, filings or statements back it up.

Sources are linked and figures are checked against them; anything I can't confirm is left out. Nothing here is personal financial advice.
